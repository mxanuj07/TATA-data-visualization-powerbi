## 📌 Project Overview

This project was completed as part of the **Tata Group Data Visualization Virtual Experience Program** on **Forage**.

The objective was to analyze retail sales data using **Power BI** and create interactive dashboards that help business leaders make data-driven decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboards answer the following business questions:

- 📈 Monthly Revenue Trends
- 🌍 Revenue and Demand by Country
- 👥 Top 10 Customers by Revenue
- 🗺️ Product Demand Across Different Countries

These insights help identify:

- High-performing markets
- Customer purchasing behavior
- Revenue trends
- Expansion opportunities

## 📈 Key Insights

- Revenue showed strong growth during the final months.
- The United Kingdom generated the highest revenue.
- The top 10 customers contributed a significant share of total revenue.
- European countries showed the highest product demand.
- Geographic visualization highlighted regions with potential for business expansion.
